Greenfield University Admissions

Undergraduate applications open on 1 March and close on 15 June each year.
Required documents: high-school transcript, ID card or passport, and a personal statement of 500 words.

The Computer Science programme requires a mathematics entrance score of at least 7.0.
International students must provide an English certificate equivalent to IELTS 6.0.

Tuition for the 2026 academic year is 28,000,000 VND per semester for local students
and 42,000,000 VND per semester for international students.

Scholarships covering 25% or 50% of tuition are awarded to students with a GPA above 3.5.
