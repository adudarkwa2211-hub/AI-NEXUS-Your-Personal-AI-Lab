Greenfield University Library

The main library is open Monday to Friday from 08:00 to 21:00.
On Saturday the library is open from 09:00 to 17:00.
The library is closed on Sunday and public holidays.

Students can borrow up to 10 books for 21 days.
Late returns cost 2,000 VND per book per day.

The quiet study zone is on the 3rd floor.
Group study rooms can be booked at the circulation desk.

Thư viện Đại học Greenfield mở cửa từ thứ Hai đến thứ Sáu, 08:00–21:00.
Thứ Bảy mở cửa 09:00–17:00. Chủ nhật đóng cửa.
